What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use our enquiry form we collect your name, email address, mobile number (if you give it), the services you are interested in, your budget and your project brief.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted suppliers who process data for us under contract:

Some of these suppliers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses.

When we build or host a website for a client, the client is the controller of any data collected through their site, and we act as their processor under our client agreement. Please contact that business directly about their privacy notice.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your data
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data
  • ask for your data in a portable format, where it applies

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
